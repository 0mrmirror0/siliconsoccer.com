MOVE ALL 3 BLOBS, EVERY TURN Drag each of your blobs to aim, then press GO

Drag from your blobs. Longer drag = harder shot. The aiming line is always straight.

2 · GO

Tap GO. Everything moves at once.

3 · SCORE

Ball in the enemy gate = point

All enemy blobs dead = point

First to 3 wins
